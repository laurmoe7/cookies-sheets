Cookie's Sheets

A character sheet for Pathfinder 1st Edition that does the math. Level up, search the rules, and keep your companions in one Windows app.

Download for Windows View the source

Live character sheet

  • Ability scores, HP, AC, saves, skills, CMB/CMD and weapons all update as you type
  • Correct bonus stacking, with buffs and conditions such as Haste, Rage and Shaken
  • Spellbooks with slots, save DCs and prepare/cast tracking
  • Portrait and name icon on the sheet
  • Pick weapons and armor from a list of about 200 standard ones, with their numbers filled in

Level-up wizard

  • Before and after numbers for HP, BAB, saves and spells per day
  • Archetypes with their class changes applied
  • Spell picking that only offers what you can learn, with a counter of what is left
  • Feats, skill ranks and favored class bonuses

Companions

  • 68 animal companion species with advancement
  • Eidolons with an evolution point tracker
  • 26 familiar kinds that scale with your master level
  • Take any of about 1,850 creatures from the Monster Codex as a companion

Compendium and quick rules

  • Search about 2,250 spells, 1,200 feats, items, classes and rules text
  • A Monster Codex of about 1,850 stat blocks, and combat stats on every standard weapon and armor
  • Cheat-sheet cards and a concentration odds calculator
  • Open any entry in a side panel straight from your sheet

Import

  • Bring in a sheet from charactersheet.co.uk or from a Google Sheet with a link or a downloaded file
  • Preview and fix class levels before saving
  • Any number the importer had to adjust to match is listed for you to check
  • Your account details from charactersheet.co.uk are never saved

Yours to keep

  • Characters are plain .json files in Documents\Pathfinder Characters
  • Works offline. The only network use is what you ask for: importing, Discord rolls and checking for updates (off until you turn it on)
  • Updates itself from inside the app, and your characters can live in any folder you choose
  • 32 classes, including APG and the Advanced Class Guide hybrids

Install

Download the zip from the latest release, unzip it anywhere and run Cookie's Sheets.exe. To run from source you need Python 3.10 or newer; see the README in the repository.
