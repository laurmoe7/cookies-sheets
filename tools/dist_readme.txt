Cookie's Sheets - a Pathfinder 1st Edition character sheet for Windows
======================================================================

HOW TO RUN
1. Unzip this folder anywhere (for example, your Documents folder).
   Keep everything together: the program needs the "_internal" folder next to it.
2. Double-click "Cookie's Sheets.exe".
3. Optional: right-click "Cookie's Sheets.exe" and choose "Pin to taskbar"
   or "Send to > Desktop (create shortcut)".

"WINDOWS PROTECTED YOUR PC"?
This app isn't from a big publisher, so Windows may warn you the first time.
Click "More info", then "Run anyway".

WHERE ARE MY CHARACTERS?
Characters save automatically to:  Documents\Pathfinder Characters
Use the Export button on the Characters page to share a character file,
and "Import .json" to load one someone sent you.

ALREADY USE charactersheet.co.uk?
On the Characters page, click "Import from charactersheet.co.uk" and paste
your sheet's link.

WHAT'S INSIDE
- Character sheet that does all the math (AC, saves, attacks, skills, spells)
- Buffs and conditions with correct bonus stacking
- Level-up wizard for 32 classes
- Searchable compendium: spells, feats, traits, races, items, rules (Ctrl+K)
- Quick-reference rules cards
- Cookie, a rude little bird who comments on everything you do (Options turns down his chatter, or bleeps the swearing)

Needs Windows 10 or 11.

TROUBLESHOOTING
- The app needs the Microsoft Edge WebView2 runtime (already part of Windows 10
  and 11). If it can't open its own window, it opens in your web browser instead.
- If something goes wrong, a note is written to
  %LOCALAPPDATA%\Cookie's Sheets\error.log  (send that file along with a bug report).
- Your settings (theme, last character) are kept in %LOCALAPPDATA%\Cookie's Sheets.
